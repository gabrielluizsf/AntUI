# Sound assets

The WAV files in `tech/` and `simple/` come from the UI SFX library,
https://github.com/romainsimon/uisfx — twelve coherent sound packs where the
`scifi` pack supplies the "Tech" template and the `arcade` pack supplies the
"Simple" one. The files were losslessly converted from Ogg Vorbis to 16-bit
mono PCM at 44100 Hz with ffmpeg; nothing else was touched.
